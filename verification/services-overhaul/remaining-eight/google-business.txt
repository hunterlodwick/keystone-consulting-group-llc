Show customers what they will find

Show the next reader how you handle feedback. We establish a response routine and escalation path for complaints, with replies specific to the experience. Private customer details stay out of public responses. A draft can save time, but someone still needs to check what happened.

Connect profile links to a useful service, booking or contact page. We check that the destination works on a phone and matches the offer. If your website leaves visitors unsure what to do, we can scope Website Design work alongside the profile improvements.

Is Google My Business the same thing?
Yes. Google Business Profile is the current name for the listing customers use in Search and Maps.
Can you recover a profile someone else controls?
We can review the ownership situation and help with Google's access process. Recovery depends on the account history and evidence; we cannot promise an approval date.
Can you remove a negative review?
We can flag content that appears to violate platform policy. A negative opinion alone is not grounds for removal. We help you respond clearly and take the underlying issue offline.
Do we need a website as well?
A profile can generate contact on its own. A useful website gives people space to inspect services and request a quote when the profile does not answer enough.
What are we paying for if the profile is free?
You pay for the agreed setup and management work. Google does not charge for the profile itself. Photo production, website work or ongoing care need their own scope.
