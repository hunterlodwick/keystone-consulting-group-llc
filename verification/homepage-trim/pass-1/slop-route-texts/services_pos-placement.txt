What We Deliver

Deep expertise across every aspect of pos placement.

Smart Terminals

Sleek, countertop-ready terminals with tap-to-pay, chip, and swipe, plus mobile wallets. Process in under 2 seconds. Dual screens for customer-facing tip prompts and receipts.

Full POS Systems

Complete point-of-sale systems with touchscreen registers, barcode scanners, receipt printers, and cash drawers. Pre-configured for your industry — restaurant, retail, salon, or service.

Kitchen Display Systems

For restaurants: digital kitchen displays that replace paper tickets. Orders route automatically from the POS to the kitchen, with bump functionality and timers, plus order prioritization.

Mobile & Wireless Readers

Take payments anywhere — tableside, at events, on the job site, or at the door. Compact wireless readers that connect via Bluetooth or WiFi and process just as fast as countertop terminals.
